# Blinkit-Sales-Dashboard
🛒 Blinkit Sales Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

The Blinkit Sales Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built in Power BI to analyze Blinkit's retail sales performance across multiple business dimensions. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor revenue, customer preferences, outlet performance, and product trends through dynamic visualizations.

Designed with an executive-level reporting approach, this dashboard helps decision-makers quickly identify key performance indicators (KPIs), compare outlet performance, and understand sales distribution across different categories.

🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide a centralized analytical dashboard that helps business users:

Monitor overall sales performance
Analyze outlet-wise revenue contribution
Compare product category performance
Evaluate customer ratings and average sales
Identify sales trends across outlet establishment years
Support data-driven business decisions through interactive filtering

📈 Key Insights
Supermarkets contribute the highest share of overall sales compared to grocery stores.
Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods are among the highest revenue-generating product categories.
Medium-sized outlets generate the largest share of total sales.
Tier 3 outlets contribute significantly to overall revenue.
Sales stabilized after a rapid increase in outlet establishment years, indicating business maturity.
Customer ratings remain consistently high across outlet types, reflecting stable customer satisfaction.
